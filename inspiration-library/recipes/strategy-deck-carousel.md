**What it is:** a long editorial carousel that shows the *thinking*, not just the design: content pillars as a donut chart, a caption framework, posting cadence bar charts, a playbook, brand-usage and wordmark rules. Rich field, elegant serif headers, clean white cards, a script wordmark sign-off, music underneath.

**Why it works:** it proves they are strategists, not just prettifiers. It is basically OUR internal brain files turned into a beautiful client-facing deliverable.

### ✅ STEAL
Turning strategy into a gorgeous carousel; serif-luxury layout; white cards on a rich field; charts as proof of thinking; music under a static carousel.

### 🔄 ADAPT
Our version uses our real frameworks. Strong post type: "here is how we would actually run [demo brand]," not just "here is a pretty logo."

### 🚫 DON'T
Copy the burgundy + cream combination one-to-one, or their Didone choice. Pick our own luxury serif.
