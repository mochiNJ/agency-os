# The palette slide done right

| | |
|---|---|
| **business type** | any |

**What it is:** a **60/30/10 donut** with named percentages, plus a stack of swatch cards each carrying a **poetic name + hex + a one-line feeling** ("dusty sage #a6b999, calm, natural and quietly grounded").

**Why it works:** the named-colour-with-a-feeling device turns a technical palette into brand storytelling, and the donut makes the hierarchy instantly legible.

### ✅ STEAL
The named-colour-with-a-feeling device and the donut.

### 🔄 ADAPT
Build it per demo house from that house's own `product.md` palette. **The biggest share must be the most legible slice**, not the least (a QC rejection we already earned on a demo carousel slide 4).

### 🚫 DON'T
Reuse their exact colours.
