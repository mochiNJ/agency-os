**What it is:** it takes ONE fictional client from *"I have an idea but don't know where to start"* all the
way to a launch-ready brand, and shows EVERYTHING an agency can do in a single post: naming, logo, palette
system, packaging and variants, lifestyle campaign photography, retail mockups, surreal art shots,
educational infographics, feed planning, content pillars, posting cadence, influencer playbook, brand
guidelines.

**Why it works:** it answers the exact anxiety the prospect has, and then over-delivers. It is basically our
internal brain files (pillars, cadence, playbook) turned into a gorgeous client-facing carousel, which is
the agency's whole "directed studio" wedge. **We already generate this strategy internally. Yours Media just
presents it beautifully.**

## The reusable slide skeleton

| # | slide | the move |

**Music runs under the whole carousel.** Elegant serif headers, cream and rich-field cards.

### 🔄 ADAPT
Fill the skeleton with the agency demo house. The wrapper slides (hook, CTA, sign-off) carry the agency's
identity; every demo-brand slide carries **that house's own palette**, read from its `product.md`. Stamping
our palette on a demo house is a QC hard-fail.

### 🚫 DON'T
Copy the burgundy-and-cream combination, the Didone choice, or the coffee subject. The structure is the
asset, not the styling.
