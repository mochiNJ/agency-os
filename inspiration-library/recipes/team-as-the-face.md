# Team as the face

| | |
|---|---|
| **business type** | creative-agency, any service brand |
| **product type** | service |
| **format** | single image or reel |
| **technique** | photography |
| **seen in** | @supersidehq ("Meet Jezmin Guerrero, Creative Designer") |

**What it is:** individual team-member spotlights, humanising an AI-powered company so it reads as people-led rather than software-led.

**Why it works:** it builds trust that a pure-AI brand cannot. Extends the founder-led-content pattern from "the founder posts" to "the visible team posts."

### ✅ STEAL
Put a real visible human behind the feed, even with a one-person operation.

### 🔄 ADAPT
Scale up to team spotlights as the company grows past one person.

### 🚫 DON'T
Manufacture fake team members, or over-produce it into corporate HR content. Keep it casual and specific: what they made, not a job-title slide.
