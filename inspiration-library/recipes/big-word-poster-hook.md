# The big-word poster hook

| | |
|---|---|
| **business type** | any |
| **product type** | any |
| **format** | single image / cover slide |
| **technique** | typography, photography |
| **seen in** | @offsetmedia.lb ("BRAND", "SEEN", "LOGO vs BRAND IDENTITY") |
| **screenshots** | [`sources/offset-media/`](../sources/offset-media/) |

**What it is:** slide 1 is one huge word or short line in heavy display type on a flat brand-colour field, or set on a real poster mounted on a sunlit textured wall with a real shadow raking across. Tiny brand logo top-center.

**Why it works:** it stops the scroll, states the benefit, and the brand colour gives instant recognition across the whole grid.

### ✅ STEAL
One dominant word, heavy type, brand-colour field OR poster-on-wall mockup with real light.

### 🔄 ADAPT
Our display face and our voice. The sunlit-poster-on-wall treatment is very reusable for cover slides. **Size it by `system/text-size-rules.md`**, never by eye.

### 🚫 DON'T
Copy their exact wording or the exact maroon.
