**What it is:** mix the SUBJECT's own fandom or object tags with the CRAFT niche tags: #donaldduck #disney #FanArt alongside #3dprinter #3dprinting, or #cupholder alongside #3dprint #bambulab.

**Why it works:** it reaches two separate audiences at once: people who love the subject, and people who follow the craft. The object's own tag catches people searching for the item, not the hobby.

### ✅ STEAL
The two-audience tag split, and always including the object's own literal name as a tag.

### 🔄 ADAPT
a client pairs the piece's subject (the client's product, a plant pot) with the printing niche tags.

### 🚫 DON'T
Name the exact town. **Public content says "Lebanon" only.**
