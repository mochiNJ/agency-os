# 3D printing lab / maker / craft manufacturing

**Who inherits this:** a client.

## The taste, in one paragraph
The **process is the product**. These accounts win on proof of craft, not on polish: a print timelapse, the
support snapping off, the finished piece turned in the hand. Captions are technical and generous, giving
away the full recipe. The audience saves rather than comments, so the post is a reference, not a moment.
