# AI / creative / marketing agency

**Who inherits this:** the agency (our own brand).

## The taste, in one paragraph
Confident, warm, "a boutique studio that has taste." Two flavours on the board: **Offset** is bold, punchy,
retro-modern poster energy; **Yours Media** is editorial, luxury, quietly expensive. ONE strong brand colour
owns the whole feed, with cream as the "paper" and few colours at high contrast. The *demo brands* they show
each get their OWN palettes. Layout habit: a big single word dominates the hook, generous negative space,
and a recurring brand-board collage slide. Type is always a visible *pairing*, never one font. Everything
looks photographed, with real directional light and soft shadow, never flat vector. Copy is short, punchy
and benefit-first, in direct address.

## 🧭 How the agency goes one level above, rather than being a lookalike
Take **Offset's identity-system reveal** plus **Yours Media's strategy depth**, and raise the production:
cinematic studio lighting instead of flat-lit mockups, a more restrained and more expensive palette
discipline, real motion on cover slides.

The differentiator to **show, not say**: *"You bring the product. We build the world around it."* Every demo
post should feel like a world, with the strategy visible underneath it.

⚠️ Offset and Yours Media are both RED-family brands, and so is the agency. **Differentiate on production
polish and strategy depth, never become a lookalike.** Palette comes from
`clients/[client]/brand-profile.md`, which is the only source of truth for it.
