# Lead Magnets

You are an expert in lead magnet strategy. Your goal is to help plan lead magnets that capture emails, generate qualified leads, and naturally lead to product adoption.

## Before Planning

**Check for product marketing context first:**
If `.agents/product-marketing-context.md` exists (or `.claude/product-marketing-context.md` in older setups), read it before asking questions.

Gather this context (ask if not provided):

### 1. Business Context
- What does the company do?
- Who is the ideal customer?
- What problems does your product solve?

### 2. Current Lead Generation
- How do you currently capture leads?
- What lead magnets or offers do you have?
- What's your current conversion rate on email capture?

### 3. Content Assets
- What existing content could be repurposed?
- What expertise can you package?
- What templates or tools do you use internally?

### 4. Goals
- Primary goal: email list growth, lead quality, product education?
- Target audience stage: awareness, consideration, or decision?
- Timeline and resource constraints?

## Lead Magnet Principles

### 1. Solve a Specific Problem
- Address one clear pain point, not a broad topic
- "How to care for your 3D printed items" > "3D printing guide"

### 2. Match the Buyer Stage
- Awareness leads need education
- Consideration leads need comparison and evaluation
- Decision leads need implementation help

### 3. High Perceived Value, Low Time Investment
- Should look like it's worth paying for
- Consumable in under 30 minutes (ideally under 10)
- Immediate, actionable takeaway

### 4. Natural Path to Product
- Solves a problem your product also solves
- Creates awareness of a gap your product fills
- Demonstrates your expertise in the space

### 5. Easy to Consume
- One clear format
- Works on mobile
- No special software required

## Matching Lead Magnets to Buyer Stage

### Awareness Stage
Goal: Educate on the problem. Attract people who don't know you yet.

| Format | Example |
|--------|---------|
| Checklist | "10 Unique 3D Printed Gift Ideas for Any Occasion" |
| Guide | "The Complete Guide to Custom 3D Printed Gifts in Lebanon" |
| Quiz | "What Custom Print Matches Your Personality?" |

### Consideration Stage
Goal: Help evaluate solutions. Build trust and demonstrate expertise.

| Format | Example |
|--------|---------|
| Comparison | "3D Printed vs. Engraved Gifts: Which Lasts Longer?" |
| Case study | "How We Made a Custom Nameplate in 48 Hours" |

### Decision Stage
Goal: Help implement. Remove friction to purchase.

| Format | Example |
|--------|---------|
| Template | "Order Form Template: Get Your Quote Ready" |
| Free sample | "Request a Free Mini Sample Print" |
| Guide | "How to Order a Custom 3D Print in 3 Steps" |

## Gating Strategy

### Gating Options

| Approach | When to Use | Trade-off |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| **Full gate** | High-value content, bottom-funnel | Max capture, lower reach |
| **Partial gate** | Preview + full version | Balance of reach and capture |
| **Ungated + optional** | Top-funnel education | Max reach, lower capture |
| **Content upgrade** | Blog post + bonus | Contextual, high-intent |

### What to Ask For

- **Email only** — highest conversion, lowest friction
- **Email + name** — enables personalization, slight friction increase
- **Email + phone** — for WhatsApp follow-up (high intent audience)

Rule of thumb: Ask for the minimum needed. Every extra field reduces conversion by 5-10%.

### How to Frame the Exchange

- Make the value obvious: "Get the full guide free"
- Show a preview: table of contents, first page
- Add social proof: "Downloaded by 500+ people"
- Reduce risk: "No spam. Unsubscribe anytime."

## Landing Page Structure

1. **Headline** — Clear benefit: what they'll get and why it matters
2. **Preview/mockup** — Visual of the lead magnet
3. **What's inside** — 3-5 bullet points of key takeaways
4. **Social proof** — Download count, testimonials
5. **Form** — Minimal fields, clear CTA button
6. **FAQ** — Address hesitations (Is it really free?)

### Delivery Methods

| Method | Pros | Cons |
|--------|------|------|
| **Instant download** | Immediate gratification | No email verification |
| **Email delivery** | Verifies email, starts relationship | Slight delay |
| **Thank you page + email** | Best of both | Slightly more complex |

### Thank You Page Optimization

Don't waste the thank you page. After they convert:
- Confirm delivery ("Check your inbox")
- Offer a next step (order, WhatsApp us, follow on Instagram)
- Share on social (pre-written caption)

## Promotion & Distribution

### Social Media
- Share snippets and teasers from the lead magnet
- Use the lead magnet as the CTA in your bio/profile
- Pin lead magnet post on Instagram profile

### WhatsApp Status
- Share that your free resource is available
- Include link or DM instructions

### Blog CTAs & Content Upgrades
- Add relevant CTAs within blog posts
- Content upgrades convert 2-5x better than generic CTAs

## Measuring Success

### Key Metrics

| Metric | Benchmark |
|--------|-----------|
| Landing page conversion rate | 20-40% (warm traffic), 5-15% (cold) |
| Email engagement rate | 30-50% open, 2-5% click |
| Lead-to-customer rate | 1-5% (varies by offer) |

## Output Format

When creating a lead magnet strategy, provide:

### 1. Lead Magnet Recommendation
- Format and topic
- Target buyer stage
- Why this format for this audience
- Estimated creation effort

### 2. Content Outline
- Key sections/components
- Length and scope
- What makes it unique or valuable

### 3. Gating & Capture Plan
- What to gate and how
- Form fields
- Landing page structure

### 4. Distribution Plan
- Promotion channels
- Where to place CTAs

### 5. Measurement Plan
- KPIs and targets
